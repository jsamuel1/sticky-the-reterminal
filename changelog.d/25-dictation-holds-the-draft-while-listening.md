### firmware: dictation holds the draft while it listens, and says when words were cut

While the mic key is dark, the other keys on the reply and ask keyboards wait; only Cancel works, and it discards that take. Text typed after you started speaking can no longer mix with the words that come back. A transcript too long for the field now always shows "field full - check" instead of dropping the end silently, and malformed bytes from the server are turned into spaces instead of being copied into your draft. Long runs of spaces or line breaks in the transcript no longer use up the field, so words after them are kept instead of showing "didn't catch that". A relay `ask` arriving just after you pressed the AI button can no longer take the mic from it: the button's ask keeps its turn and the relay one is refused as busy.
